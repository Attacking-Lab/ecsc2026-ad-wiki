# Contact

## Responsible Disclosure

Please report vulnerabilities you find in the platform to the organizers via
the ticket bot in the ECSC 2026 Discord server.

In the unlikely case that Discord is unavailable, please send an email to the following address:
<a href="mailto:ecsc2026+bugs@attacking-lab.com">ecsc2026+bugs@attacking-lab.com</a>

## Bug Bounty

<span class=hltext>We want to reward players who find and report bugs in our platform.</span>

If you report a bug before the competition and help us triage, we will do our best to reward
that show of sportsmanship.
